Efficient Frequent Pattern Mining for Large Scale Transaction Databases

This research investigates frequent pattern mining techniques for large scale transaction
databases. Association rule mining and the Apriori algorithm are reviewed, and an improved
candidate generation strategy is proposed to reduce the number of database scans required.
Experiments on retail transaction datasets show that the proposed pruning strategy reduces
runtime while preserving the completeness of the discovered frequent itemsets. Data mining
of large repositories continues to be an important area for extracting actionable knowledge
from transactional records, and pattern mining remains a core building block of that process.
