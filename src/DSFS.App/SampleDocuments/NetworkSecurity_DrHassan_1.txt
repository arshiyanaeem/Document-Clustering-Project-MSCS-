An Intrusion Detection Framework for Wireless Sensor Networks

Wireless sensor networks are vulnerable to a range of security attacks including sinkhole,
wormhole and denial of service attacks due to their limited energy and computational resources.
This paper proposes a lightweight intrusion detection framework for wireless sensor networks
that monitors routing behaviour and flags nodes exhibiting anomalous packet forwarding patterns.
The framework is evaluated through simulation of a multi hop sensor network under several attack
scenarios. Results show that the proposed network security mechanism achieves high detection
accuracy while incurring low communication overhead, making it suitable for resource constrained
sensor network deployments.
